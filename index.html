How the Garden Works

🌱

Plant

Pick a seed from the dock, then tap an empty plot. Plants grow from seed to sprout to bloom.

🎶

Sing

Tap a blooming plant to hear its voice. Each species sings its own syllables.

💧

Water

Switch to Water mode and tap a plant. Watered plants sing richer harmonies.

✍

Conduct

Switch to Conduct mode, then drag across plants to make them sing in sequence.

🏔

Scenes

Tap the flower icon in the top bar (or press N) to move the garden between meadow, sakura grove, desert oasis and snowfield.

♫

Harmony & Chaos

Harmony sings everyone in tune; Chaos lets everyone improvise at once.

🤣

Personality

Your plants sneeze, cough, gossip and hum. Overwater one and the sneeze spreads to its neighbours.
