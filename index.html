Singing Garden

Welcome to Aurora Bloom

🌱

Plant

Pick a glowing seed from the dock, then tap an empty plot. Plants grow from seed to sprout to bloom.

🎶

Sing

Tap a blooming plant to hear its voice. Each species sings a different syllable.

💧

Water

Switch to Water mode and tap a plant. Watered plants sing richer harmonies.

✍

Conduct

Switch to Conduct mode, then drag across plants to make them sing in sequence — your own melody.

🌙

Day / Night

Toggle the sky. Night makes the garden glow brighter and adds an ambient pad.

♫

Harmony & Chaos

Harmony sings everyone in tune; Chaos lets everyone improvise at once.
