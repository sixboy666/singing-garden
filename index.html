How to Play

🌱

Plant

Choose a plant from the bottom menu, then click an empty plot to plant it.

🎵

Sing

Click a planted flower to make it sing! Each plant has its own unique voice.

💧

Water

Toggle Water mode, then click a plant to water it. Watering changes how it sounds!

🎼

Melody

Plant different flowers and press Play Melody to hear them sing together!

🤣

Chaos!

Every plant sings at once in a gloriously absurd choir. Nobody is in tune. That's the point.
